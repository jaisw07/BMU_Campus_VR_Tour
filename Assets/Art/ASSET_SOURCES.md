## Trees (Polyhaven, CC0)

| Asset | URL | Author | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jacaranda Tree (2K glTF) | https://polyhaven.com/a/jacaranda_tree | Rico Cilliers (model), Rob Tuytel (guidance) | `Assets/Models/Trees/Jacaranda/`. The raw glTF is about 3.6M triangles with a 208 MB `.bin`, so the `.bin` is git-ignored. The baked LOD meshes (`Jacaranda_LOD0..2.asset`), billboards, materials and prefabs are what the scene uses. To re-bake, download the 2K glTF from the link above into that folder (or run `Assets/Art/fetch_assets.py` equivalent steps) and use *BMU VR Tour > Realism > Bake Jacaranda Trees*. |

Requested but not obtainable automatically: the three CGTrader models (PBR Peach Tree Collection, XfrogPlants Honey Locust,
Oak Tree). CGTrader needs an account login to download, so they were not fetched. Drop their files into
`Assets/Models/Trees/{PeachTree,HoneyLocust,Oak}` and they can be run through the same LOD/prefab pipeline.

## Generated in-project (no external source)

- Grass clump, weed and flower cards (`Assets/Art/Textures/Vegetation/`): drawn procedurally by `RealismTerrain.cs`.
- Road decals (cracks, patches, tyre wear, edge dirt): drawn procedurally by `RealismRoads.cs`.
- Street furniture (lamp posts, bollards, benches, low walls): simple meshes built by `RealismRoads.cs`.
- Tree billboard textures: rendered from the Jacaranda LOD mesh by `RealismTrees.cs`.
- `Assets/Shaders/BMU_Foliage.shader`: project shader (cutout, wind sway, translucency).
